Mission

How we run the group.

These are the priorities we use when planning rides, events, and member support.

01

Make rides welcoming

Respect names, pronouns, boundaries, and different riding backgrounds.

02

Ride responsibly

Plan clearly, communicate expectations, and prioritize safety on every ride.

03

Share knowledge

Help newer riders learn and make riding more accessible over time.

Member-led

Members can revise this as the alliance grows.

Priorities will keep evolving based on what members need from rides, leadership, and events.
