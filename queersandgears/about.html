About us

Who we are and how we ride.

Queers & Gears brings together LGBTQ+ motorcyclists, passengers, future riders, and allies around motorcycles and community.

Who we are

We are a queer-led motorcycle alliance built on respect and shared responsibility.

Our gatherings are a chance to ride, learn, and build friendships across identities, backgrounds, and experience levels.

Motorcycles bring us together; people keep us coming back.

Our values

What matters on every ride.

Respect first

We respect names, pronouns, boundaries, skill levels, and lived experiences.

Safety always

We prioritize responsible riding, clear communication, and looking out for one another.

Community care

We share knowledge, celebrate one another, and help make riding more accessible.
