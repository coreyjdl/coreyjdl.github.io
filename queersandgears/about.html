About Queers & Gears.

Queers & Gears is a queer-led motorcycle alliance for riders, passengers, future riders, and allies.

What the group is about

We run respectful rides, share riding knowledge, and make it easier for people to join in.

Members include experienced riders, new riders, passengers, and supporters.

Our values

What matters on every ride.

Respect first

We respect names, pronouns, boundaries, skill levels, and lived experiences.

Safety always

We prioritize responsible riding, clear communication, and looking out for one another.

Community care

We share knowledge, celebrate one another, and help make riding more accessible.
