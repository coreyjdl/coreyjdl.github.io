About the alliance

Queers & Gears is a queer-led motorcycle group for riders, passengers, future riders, and allies.

What we're here for

We organize rides, share riding knowledge, and make it easier for people to find motorcycle community without having to fit a particular mold.

Members include experienced riders, new riders, passengers, people who are still learning, and supporters.

What matters to us

Respect

Names, pronouns, boundaries, skill levels, and lived experiences matter.

Safety

Clear communication and responsible riding come before pace or ego.

Community

We share knowledge, help each other out, and try to make motorcycling easier to get into.
