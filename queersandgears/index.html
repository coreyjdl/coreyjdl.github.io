Queers & Gears Motorcycle Alliance is a community for LGBTQ+ riders, passengers, friends, and allies who believe the road is better when everyone belongs.

Everyone has a place

Your bike. Your journey. Your community.

You don’t need a particular kind of motorcycle—or even your own motorcycle—to connect with Queers & Gears.

Whether you’ve been riding for decades, are studying for your first endorsement, prefer the passenger seat, or simply support the community, there’s room for you here.
